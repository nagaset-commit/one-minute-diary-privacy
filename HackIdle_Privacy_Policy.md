# HackIdle Privacy Policy

nagaset-commit (“Developer”) establishes this Privacy Policy for the handling of user information in the iOS application “HackIdle” (“App”).

## 1. Information Stored on the Device

The App stores the following information on the user’s device to provide its game features:

- Dungeon and sector progress
- Party composition, character positions, and combat settings
- Equipment inventory, equipped items, locks, temporary storage, and auto-salvage settings
- Saved builds, simulation comparison history, and equipment-goal history
- Facility levels, research focus, salvage resources, stamina, and idle-reward status
- Enemy codex discoveries
- Daily optional-ad usage and pending ad bonuses
- Last-played time, idle-start time, and other timestamps required for progression
- App settings

This information is used for game progression, save data, idle-reward calculations, equipment management, history and statistics, and maintaining settings.

This information is stored on the user’s device and is not sent to the Developer’s server. Deleting the App generally deletes this locally stored information.

## 2. Local Notifications

With the user’s permission, the App uses iOS local notifications to warn when idle-reward items in temporary storage are approaching expiration.

Notification schedules, expiration times, and other information required for notifications are processed on the device and are not sent to the Developer’s server. Notification permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

## 3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display optional rewarded advertisements. The App does not show forced advertisements and displays an advertisement only when the user chooses to watch one.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following information for ad delivery, ad-performance measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement:

- IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
- Device identifiers, app- or developer-bounded identifiers, and advertising identifiers
- Information about advertisements shown
- Interactions with advertisements and the App
- App launch time, operating status, and performance information
- Crash logs and other diagnostic information

The information actually collected depends on the user’s consent choices, device settings, region, and the advertisements served.

For details about Google’s handling of information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information for advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure)

## 4. Advertising Consent and Choices

In regions where consent or privacy choices are required, the App uses Google User Messaging Platform to display the applicable advertising privacy message.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and applicable US states, users can review or change their consent or opt-out choices through “Ad privacy settings” when that option is available in the App.

Not watching advertisements or declining ad personalization does not change the App’s game content or standard rewards. Additional rewards for optional advertisements are granted only after completion of an advertisement has been confirmed.

## 5. Tracking

If the App uses an advertising identifier for ad personalization or measurement, it requests permission under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework.

The App’s game features and optional advertisements remain available if the user does not allow tracking. In that case, the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not include the IDFA in ad requests. Tracking permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

## 6. In-App Purchases

The App uses Apple StoreKit to provide in-app purchases and restore purchase status.

Apple manages purchase processing, payment information, and transaction information. The App checks the product identifier, purchase validity, and revocation status required to provide purchased features. The Developer does not receive the user’s credit-card number or other payment-method details.

For details about Apple’s handling of information, see the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 7. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Developer does not independently sell or provide user information to third parties except:

- When using external services described in this Policy, such as advertising or in-app purchases
- With the user’s consent
- When disclosure is required by law
- When necessary to protect a person’s life, body, or property

Processing, sharing, and international transfers by external service providers are governed by each provider’s privacy policy.

## 8. Information Managed by External Services

Information collected by Google AdMob or Apple is managed according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

The Developer may not be able to directly access, correct, or delete information managed by these services. Please refer to each provider’s procedures for requests relating to information they manage.

## 9. User Controls

Users can manage information and permissions related to the App as follows:

- Local game data: Delete the App
- Advertising consent and opt-out choices: Use “Ad privacy settings” in the App
- Tracking permission: Use the privacy settings in iOS Settings
- Notification permission: Use notification settings in iOS Settings
- Information managed by Apple: Use [Apple Data and Privacy](https://privacy.apple.com/)
- Google advertising settings: Use [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

## 10. Security

The Developer takes reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or alteration of information handled by the App.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be revised in response to changes in law, App features, or external services used by the App.

Material changes will be announced in the App or on the web page where this Policy is published.
